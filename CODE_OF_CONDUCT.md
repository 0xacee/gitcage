# Code of conduct

GitCage contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe and
productive.

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique code and claims, not people or identities.
- Do not expose another person's credentials, account associations, employer,
  private repositories, or local paths.
- Do not use project spaces for harassment, impersonation, or coordinated abuse.
- Assume good faith while still requiring evidence for security claims.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, or restrict participation
when behavior threatens contributors or users. Serious incidents can be reported
through the private channel described in `SECURITY.md`.
